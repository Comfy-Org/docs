Credits power **Partner Nodes** (API calls to closed-source models). ComfyUI stays fully open-source and free for local graph execution; credits only apply when you run Partner Nodes or use Cloud billing features.

After you sign in under **Settings → User**, open **Settings → Workspace → Plan & Credits**. On Comfy Cloud you can also open the same panel from the profile menu with **Manage plan**.

![ComfyUI Plan & Credits](/images/interface/setting/menu-credits.jpg)

## What’s in Plan & Credits

| Tab | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Credits** | Balance, top-up, and plan summary (local embeds the credits panel; Cloud shows the workspace subscription view) |
| **Activity** | Usage history for credit spend |
| **Invoices** | Cloud only, for users who can manage the workspace subscription — next invoice and Stripe history |

Detailed steps:

<Steps>

<Step title="Log in to your ComfyUI account">
Open **Settings → User** and sign in.
![Login Interface](/images/interface/setting/user.jpg)
</Step>

<Step title="Open Plan & Credits">
After login, open **Settings → Workspace → Plan & Credits** (or Cloud profile → **Manage plan**).
![Plan & Credits](/images/interface/setting/purchase-1.jpg)
</Step>

<Step title="Set the amount of credits to purchase">
![Set Purchase Amount](/images/interface/setting/buy.jpg)
In the popup, set the purchase amount and click **Buy**.
</Step>

<Step title="Make payment through Stripe">
![Stripe Payment Page](/images/interface/setting/stripe_payment.jpg)
On the payment page:
1. Select the currency for payment
2. Confirm that the email matches your ComfyUI account email
3. Choose your payment method
   - Credit Card
   - WeChat (only when paying in Comfy Credits)
   - Alipay (only when paying in Comfy Credits)
4. Click **Pay** or **Generate QR Code** to finish
</Step>

<Step title="Confirm your balance">
![Payment Successful](/images/interface/setting/purchase-2.jpg)
Return to **Plan & Credits** and check that the balance updated. Refresh or restart if needed.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can credits go negative?">
    Credits are not intended as a credit line. Partner Nodes sometimes report cost after execution, so one run can leave a temporary negative balance. While the balance is negative, Partner Nodes stay blocked until you top up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund for unused credits?">
  Currently we do not support refunds for unused credits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I check my current balance and usage?">
  Open **Settings → Workspace → Plan & Credits**. Use the **Credits** tab for balance and the **Activity** tab for usage history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I share my credits with other users?">
  You can sign into the same account on multiple devices. Sharing credits with other user accounts is not supported. On Cloud Team workspaces, members share the workspace balance under owner-set limits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I know how much the node will cost?">
  Check the price badge on the API / Partner Node before running. Actual cost can vary with image size, generation count, and other parameters.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why don't I see WeChat or Alipay payment options?">
  Choose payment in Comfy Credits. WeChat and Alipay are only available for that currency path.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do credits expire?">
  Yes, expiration depends on the type:
  - **Monthly credits**: Expire at the end of your billing period
  - **Top-up credits**: Expire 1 year from the purchase date
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
