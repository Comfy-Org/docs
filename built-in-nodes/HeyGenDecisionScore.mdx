Rate the text, images, audio or video you connect from 1 to 10, and get the score plus a boolean that flips at a threshold you set. Use it to filter or sort outputs, and connect `result` to an If/Else Switch node to act on the score.

## Inputs

| Parameter | Description | Data Type | Required | Range |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|----------|-------|
| `model` | Model to use. `heygen-decision-1-turbo` is faster and costs less than `heygen-decision-1` (default: `"heygen-decision-1"`). | COMBO | Yes | `"heygen-decision-1"`<br />`"heygen-decision-1-turbo"` |
| `question` | What to rate, saying what 1 and 10 mean (default: "Rate the technical quality of the image from 1 (worst) to 10 (best)."). | STRING | Yes | 1 to 5000 characters |
| `threshold` | `result` is true when the score is at least this value. Clean, sharp images score about 7, badly damaged ones about 3 to 5 (default: 6.0). | FLOAT | Yes | 1.0 to 10.0 (step 0.1) |
| `images` | Images to rate, up to 4 in total, counting every frame of a batch. They are numbered `image_1` to `image_4` in order across the sockets. | IMAGE | No | Up to 4 images |
| `audio_1` | Audio to rate, up to 30 minutes. | AUDIO | No | Up to 30 minutes |
| `video_1` | Video to rate, up to 60 seconds. | VIDEO | No | Up to 60 seconds |
| `text` | Text the question is about, such as a prompt or an LLM response, up to 20000 characters. | STRING | No | Up to 20000 characters |

Connect at least one input to rate and name them by socket inside the question, for example "How sharp is image_1?".

## Outputs

| Output Name | Description | Data Type |
|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| `result` | True when the score is at least `threshold`, otherwise false. | BOOLEAN |
| `score` | The rating from 1 to 10. | FLOAT |
