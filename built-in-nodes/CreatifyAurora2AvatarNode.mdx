Creatify Aurora 2.0 Avatar turns one photo plus a speech or song clip into a talking or singing video. The video is as long as the connected audio and keeps that audio unchanged.

## Inputs

| Parameter | Description | Data Type | Required | Range |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|----------|-------|
| `image` | Photo of the person to animate. At least 256 pixels on each side and an aspect ratio between 1:4 and 4:1; the video keeps the photo's aspect ratio. | IMAGE | Yes | - |
| `audio` | Speech or song that drives the mouth and the length of the video. Up to 60 seconds, or 14 seconds at `"2k"`; billed per started second. | AUDIO | Yes | Up to 60 seconds |
| `resolution` | Resolution of the output video (default: `"720p"`). | COMBO | Yes | `"480p"`<br />`"720p"`<br />`"1080p"`<br />`"2k"` |
| `text_prompt` | Scene, camera, lighting, expression or motion for the shot. Empty keeps the photo as it is (default: empty string). | STRING | Yes | Any text |
| `seed` | Seed controls whether the node re-runs. Results are non-deterministic regardless of seed (default: 42). | INT | Yes | 0 to 2147483647 |

**Constraints:** a photo smaller than 256 pixels on a side, or with an aspect ratio beyond 1:4 to 4:1, raises an error. Audio longer than the limit for the chosen resolution is rejected: 60 seconds normally and 14 seconds at `"2k"`.

## Outputs

| Output Name | Description | Data Type |
|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| `VIDEO` | The talking or singing video, with the connected audio. | VIDEO |
