Upscale a video with Clarity AI's Crystal Video Upscaler, a high-fidelity upscaler that stays faithful to the source. The clip is uploaded, upscaled by the factor you set, and returned as a video. The output is capped at 9 megapixels per frame (for example 4000x2250) and at 360 megapixel-seconds per clip, so large or long clips come back at a lower resolution than the factor asks for.

Selecting a `model` reveals the parameters specific to that model.

## Inputs

| Parameter | Description | Data Type | Required | Range |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|----------|-------|
| `model` | Model to use. Selecting a model reveals the parameters specific to it: `video` and `scale_factor`. | DYNAMIC_COMBO | Yes | `"crystal-video-upscaler"` |
| `video` | The video to upscale. | VIDEO | Yes | N/A |
| `scale_factor` | Factor to multiply the video width and height by. The output is limited to 9 megapixels per frame and 360 megapixel-seconds per clip, so large or long videos are upscaled by less, and the factor is reduced again when it would push a side past 5120 pixels (default: 2.0). | FLOAT | Yes | 1.0 to 200.0 (step 0.1) |

**Note:** The clip may be at most 5120 pixels on each side and at most 9 megapixels per frame. The maximum length depends on the frame size and frame rate: it is 360 megapixel-seconds divided by the frame pixels and by the frame rate rounded up to a multiple of 30 fps, so a 1080p clip at 30 fps can run up to about 173 seconds. Clips above 30 fps cost 2x, above 60 fps 3x, and so on.

## Outputs

| Output Name | Description | Data Type |
|-------------|-------------|-----------|
| `video` | The upscaled video. | VIDEO |
