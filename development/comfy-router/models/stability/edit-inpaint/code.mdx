### Input

<ParamField body="grow_mask" type="integer">
  How far, in pixels, to grow the mask. Default 5.

  Range: `0` to `100`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="image" type="string (uri)" required>
  A Comfy-signed asset URL of the source image.

  Format: `uri`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="mask" type="string (uri)">
  A Comfy-signed asset URL of the mask. White marks the area to repaint. Omitted, the image's own alpha is the mask.

  Format: `uri`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="negative_prompt" type="string">
  What to keep out of the painted area.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="output_format" type="string">
  Only `png` is returned.

  Possible values: `png`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="prompt" type="string" required>
  What to paint into the masked area.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="seed" type="integer">
  0 or omitted picks a random seed.

  Range: `0` to `4294967294`
</ParamField>
