## Schema

### Input

<ParamField body="base_thickness_mm" type="number">
  Base plate thickness in millimeters, 0.1 to 10. With remove_base true, omit it or send 0.

  Range: `0` to `10`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="bend_angle" type="number" default="60">
  Bend angle in degrees. Below 1 the relief stays flat.

  Range: `0` to `360`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="bend_axis" type="string">
  `xz` (default, bends the left and right edges) or `yz` (bends the top and bottom edges). Applies only when bend_mode is convex or concave.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="bend_mode" type="string">
  `off` (default), `convex` or `concave`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="carving_depth_mm" type="number">
  Carving depth in millimeters. The default is 6 for variant high and 3 for variant low.

  Range: `0` to `60`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="carving_mode" type="string">
  `emboss` (default) or `engrave`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="color_mapping" type="boolean" default="false">
  Color the model from the image. GLB, OBJ, FBX and USDZ carry the color; STL and 3MF stay white. The cost does not change.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="image_url" type="string" required>
  A public URL or data URI of a jpg, jpeg, png or webp image. At most 20 MB and at least 32 x 32 pixels. Transparent areas are filled with black.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="mesh_resolution" type="integer" default="1024">
  Points along the longer side. Larger values give much larger files (about 150 MB GLB and 300 MB STL at 2048).

  Range: `512` to `2048`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="removal_threshold" type="number" default="0.01">
  Background removal threshold. Applies only when remove_base is true.

  Range: `0.01` to `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="remove_base" type="boolean" default="false">
  Cut away the background so the relief has no base plate.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="should_generate_model" type="boolean" default="true">
  Set to false for a depth map only, with no model and no preview. The cost does not change.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="size_mm" type="number" default="60">
  Length of the longer side in millimeters.

  Range: `20` to `250`
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="target_formats" type="string[]">
  The model formats to generate. One or more of glb, obj, fbx, stl, usdz and 3mf. The default is glb and stl.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="variant" type="string">
  `low` (default) gives slight depth and finer detail, for laser or CNC work. `high` gives deeper, sculptural relief, for 3D printing. It also sets the default carving_depth_mm.
</ParamField>
