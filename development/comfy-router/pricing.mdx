---

Comfy Router charges the model maker's list price. Each call is billed from your workspace credits, and 211 credits = $1 USD.

<Info>
**Comfy Router pricing has moved to [router.comfy.org](https://router.comfy.org).** Check current prices and compare models there.
</Info>

<Card title="See Router prices on router.comfy.org" icon="coins" href="https://router.comfy.org">
  Current per-model prices for Comfy Router.
</Card>

Unpublished prices do not mean free usage.

For per-model billing facts, such as whether a policy refusal is charged, read the `billing` field from `GET /v2/models` or the model detail response. It does not carry prices. To price a specific call before you run it, use `POST /v2/models/{provider}/{model}/estimate`. See [Billing](/development/comfy-router/billing#model-billing-facts) and the [API reference](/development/comfy-router/reference).
