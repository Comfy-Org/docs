Kling 4.0 is Kling AI's video generation model, available in ComfyUI as Partner Nodes. It renders clips up to 30 seconds long at 720p or 1080p and produces stereo audio in the same pass as the picture, so ambience, impacts, and dialogue arrive with the frames instead of needing a separate sound pass.

Four nodes cover the workflows: **Kling 4.0 Text to Video**, **Kling 4.0 Image to Video**, **Kling 4.0 Reference to Video**, and **Kling 4.0 Edit Video**. The image node spends one keyframe budget of ten images, Reference to Video adds up to five reference clips, and Edit Video rewrites a clip you already have instead of generating a new one.

All four are cloud API nodes. They need a Comfy account with credits, download no model files, and bill per second of video. See [Partner Node pricing](/tutorials/partner-nodes/pricing) for the current rates.

## What Kling 4.0 is good at

- **Long single takes**: up to 30 seconds in one generation, with the action and the camera holding together across the whole clip
- **Keyframe control**: up to 10 images pin the look at chosen moments, so a shot gets shaped instead of re-rolled
- **Reference-guided casting and locations**: up to 10 images and 5 clips carry a character, a product, or a room into a new shot
- **Edits that keep the rest**: name the change and list what must survive, and the performance, shot count, and timing stay put
- **Audio in the same pass**: stereo audio is generated with the picture
- **Multi-shot direction**: cuts and camera moves are described in the prompt and generated inside one clip

## Available workflows

### Kling 4.0: Text to Video {#api_kling4_t2v}

Generate from the prompt alone. Describe the action beat by beat, because one generation can hold several shots and several camera moves.

**How to use**

1. Write the prompt with the shots in order, each with its own camera move.
2. Set `duration` (3 to 30 seconds), `resolution`, and `aspect_ratio`.
3. `seed` only decides whether the node re-runs. Results vary between runs at the same seed.

### Kling 4.0: Image to Video {#api_kling4_i2v}

Animate stills into one clip. Connect a `first_frame`, up to eight `middle_frames`, and an optional `last_frame`, which together stay inside the ten-image budget. Middle frames are spaced evenly across the duration, and the clip takes the aspect ratio of the first keyframe.

### Kling 4.0: Reference to Video {#api_kling4_r2v}

Shoot a new clip from references instead of from one starting frame. Connect up to 10 `reference_images` and up to 5 `reference_videos`; `first_frame` and `last_frame` are optional and count toward the ten-image limit. `aspect_ratio` gains `adaptive`, which follows the prompt, then the first frame or the reference clips, and falls back to `16:9`.

### Kling 4.0: Video Edit {#api_kling4_video_edit}

Rewrite a clip you already have. `video_1` takes a 3 to 30 second clip, and the output keeps its length and, on `adaptive`, its aspect ratio. Up to four more reference clips and up to 10 reference images can be connected when the new element has to look like something specific, such as a garment, a product, or a location.

## Node controls

| Control | Values | Applies to | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `prompt` | text, up to 26,000 characters | all four nodes | The shot description. Reference images and videos are `@Image1`, `@Video1` in connection order. On Reference to Video a connected keyframe is `@first_frame` or `@last_frame`, and `@ImageN` counts reference images only |
| `resolution` | `720p`, `1080p` | all four nodes | Output resolution |
| `duration` | 3 to 30 seconds, 5 by default | Text, Image, and Reference to Video | Output length. Edit Video keeps the length of `video_1` instead |
| `aspect_ratio` | `16:9`, `9:16`, `1:1`, `21:9`; `adaptive` on Reference to Video and Edit Video | all four nodes | Output shape. Image to Video takes the ratio of the first keyframe |
| `first_frame`, `middle_frames`, `last_frame` | up to 10 images in total | Image to Video | Keyframes the clip passes through. Middles are spaced evenly |
| `reference_images` | up to 10 images | Reference to Video, Edit Video | Character, product, or location references. Aspect ratio of each image between 2:5 and 5:2 |
| `reference_videos` | up to 5 clips on Reference to Video, up to 4 on Edit Video | Reference to Video, Edit Video | Each clip 3 to 30 seconds, all clips together at most 30 seconds |
| `video_1` | one clip, 3 to 30 seconds | Edit Video | The clip being edited |
| `seed` | integer, 42 by default | all four nodes | Re-run control only. Output is non-deterministic regardless of the seed |

## Prompting tips

- **Give each shot its own camera move.** Left unspecified, several moves average into one slow drift. Name every cut and every move.
- **Name what each reference owns.** With `@Image1` for the person and `@Image2` for the room, pin the attributes: `keep the copper bob, the trench and the boots exactly as they are`, and for the location `do not change the hall`.
- **Say where the subject stands.** A reference pins identity and place, not blocking. Write the new position in words, otherwise every cut lands on the framing the reference already had.
- **Open an edit with `Edit @Video1:`.** Everything after the change is a keep list. Listing what must survive is what stops the model from quietly re-staging the shot.
- **Describe speed as evidence.** A dash reads as fast when the prompt says what moves and how far, for example crossing most of the frame in 3 to 5 frames and then stopping dead for 2 to 4 frames.
- **Keep one idea per shot.** Few shots with one clear beat each survive better than a long list of beats packed into the same clip.

## Limits

- Images: at least 300x300 px, aspect ratio between 2:5 and 5:2, at most 10 per run.
- Videos: each 3 to 30 seconds, all connected clips together at most 30 seconds, at least 24 fps, 700 to 4553 px per side, up to 8,294,400 px in total (3840x2160), aspect ratio between 2:5 and 5:2.
- Prompt references must resolve: an `@ImageN` or `@VideoN` with no matching connected input fails the run.
