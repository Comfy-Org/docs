Kling 4.0 is Kling AI's video and image generation model, available in ComfyUI as Partner Nodes. One video generation runs up to 30 seconds at 720p or 1080p and produces stereo audio alongside the picture. The image nodes render stills up to 8K.

Four video templates and three image templates cover the workflows below. Each video node also offers a faster `kling-4.0-flash` model option, limited to 720p and 20 seconds.

## Kling 4.0: Image to Video {#api_kling4_i2v}

Animates one still, or a sequence of keyframes: an optional first frame, up to eight middle frames, and an optional last frame, ten images in total. The clip takes the aspect ratio of the first keyframe. `kling-4.0-flash` animates a single first frame.

## Kling 4.0: Reference to Video {#api_kling4_r2v}

## Kling 4.0: Text to Image {#api_kling4_image_t2i}

Renders one image from a prompt at 2K, 4K, or 8K in ten aspect ratios. Connect up to ten reference images and name them in the prompt as `@Image1` onward to steer the result. Set `series_amount` to `auto` or a number from 2 to 10 to get a set of related images that keep the same characters and style.

## Kling 4.0: Image Edit {#api_kling4_image_edit}

Restyles an image while keeping what must not change. Connect the photo as the first reference image, then describe both the change and what stays: the template keeps the face, the framing, and the backdrop, and changes only the hair, makeup, and outfit. The node is the same as Text to Image, with a reference image connected.

## Kling 4.0: Long Image {#api_kling4_long_image}

Renders one very tall image in a single pass. Pick the kind of page with `skill`:

- `webpage`: optional style and subject images, addressed as `@Image1` and `@Image2`. Describe the page band by band, and state the language of the visible copy.
- `ecommerce`: one to three product images, addressed as `@Image1` onward, and a `length` of `short`, `medium`, or `long`.

Both skills render at 1K or 2K.

## Input limits

- Images: at least 300 x 300 px, aspect ratio between 2:5 and 5:2. The image nodes take up to ten reference images, and Long Image takes one to three product images in `ecommerce` mode.
- Videos: each 3 to 30 seconds (3 to 20 with `kling-4.0-flash`), all connected clips 30 seconds in total, at least 24 fps, 700 to 4553 px per side, at most 8,294,400 px in total, aspect ratio between 2:5 and 5:2.
- In the image, reference, and edit nodes, a prompt that names `@ImageN` or `@VideoN` beyond the number of connected inputs fails the run.

Every parameter is listed on the node reference pages: [Text to Video](/built-in-nodes/Kling4TextToVideoNode), [Image to Video](/built-in-nodes/Kling4ImageToVideoNode), [Reference to Video](/built-in-nodes/Kling4ReferenceToVideoNode), [Edit Video](/built-in-nodes/Kling4EditVideoNode).
