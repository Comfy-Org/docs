MAGI-2 is Sand.ai's video model, available in ComfyUI as three Partner Nodes. Every run returns a 1080p clip of 4 to 30 seconds with native audio, so dialogue, foley, and score arrive with the picture.

## What MAGI-2 is good at

- **Instruction following**: crowded scenes, several subjects, and long shots keep the layout you describe
- **Native audio**: sound is generated in the same pass as the video, no separate audio step
- **First and last frame**: animate toward a chosen end frame, not only away from a start frame
- **Reference consistency**: hold a character, location, camera move, or voice across shots

1. Write the prompt. Describe the scene, the action, and the sound you want
2. Set `duration` (4 to 30 seconds) and `aspect_ratio` (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, or 21:9)
3. Leave `generate_audio` on to get sound with the video
4. Click **Run**

Output resolution is 1080p. Changing the seed re-runs the node, but results vary even with the same seed.

1. Connect reference images, videos, or audio clips. The node accepts 10 images, 5 videos, and 5 audio clips
2. Refer to them in the prompt as `@Image1`, `@Video1`, and `@Audio1`. Numbering restarts per type and follows input order
3. Set `duration` and `aspect_ratio`, then click **Run**

References have limits: videos must be at least 2 seconds, each type is trimmed to 30 seconds in total, and audio shorter than 2 seconds is padded with silence.
