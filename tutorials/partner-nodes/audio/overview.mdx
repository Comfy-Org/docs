This page helps you pick the right model for text-to-audio, music, sound effects, or video scoring.

## What you can do with audio models

- **Text-to-audio**: Generate music or sound effects from a text description
- **Video-to-music**: Create a soundtrack that matches a video's content and pacing
- **Voice & speech**: Generate speech or singing from text and reference audio

## Choosing a model by task

### Music & soundtracks

- **Seed Audio 1.0** (ByteDance) — text-to-audio and text-to-music generation, including instrumental and vocal tracks
- **Sonilo** (Sonilo) — generate a full soundtrack from a video: it analyzes the video's rhythm and content to produce a matching score

### Sound effects & audio cleanup

- **Mirelo SFX 2.0** (Mirelo): sound effects from a text description, or Foley timed to the action in a clip, with the dialogue returned on a separate track
- **VEED Clean Audio** (VEED): removes noise, music, and effects from speech, on a recording or on a video's soundtrack
- **Seed Audio 1.0** (ByteDance) — also supports sound effects and audio-style transfer
