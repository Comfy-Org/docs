Six MiniMax H3 Max editing nodes take a video or a still image as their starting point and edit that material. They run through your Comfy API account like the other H3 API nodes, and were added in ComfyUI 0.44.0.

Three of them ship with workflow templates: **Cast Swap** (`api_minimax_h3_max_recast`), **Aspect Ratio Reframe** (`api_minimax_h3_max_reframe`) and **Camera Control** (`api_minimax_h3_max_camera_control`). The other three are in the node library under **Partner Nodes > video > MiniMax**, ready to drop into your own workflow. The first two are walked through below; the [Camera Control template](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/blob/main/templates/api_minimax_h3_max_camera_control.json) orbits a still image and lays a music bed under the clip.

For generation with MiniMax H3, H3 Max, and H3 Max Turbo, see [MiniMax H3 API Video Generation](/tutorials/partner-nodes/minimax/minimax-h3).

## MiniMax H3 Max: Cast Swap

Replace the people in a finished clip without a reshoot: the node keeps the motion, the framing, the cuts and the original audio, and generates the new people from your reference photos.

Load your own clip and connect one to four photos, one per new person. By default the photos replace the main people in the clip from left to right, so connect them in the order you want. The prompt is optional: name the photos as `@Image1` to `@Image4` and say who becomes whom, plus anything that has to stay the same.

- **Source clip**: 5 to 30 seconds, with no single shot longer than 15 seconds, and an aspect ratio between 0.4 and 2.5
- **Reference photos**: at least 256 x 256 pixels, up to four
- **Resolution**: 768P or 1080P
- **Enhance realism**: off by default. Turn it on for more natural skin texture and a better blend into the scene

## MiniMax H3 Max: Aspect Ratio Reframe

Fit a clip to a different aspect ratio without cropping the subject. The source keeps its position, timing and audio on the new canvas, and the model generates the surrounding picture.

The bundled demo turns a vertical portrait clip into a 16:9 frame. Load your own clip, pick the target aspect ratio and resolution, and use the prompt to describe what fills the new area, for example continuing the background that is already behind the subject on both sides.

- **Source clip**: 0.5 to 300 seconds
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 21:9 or 9:21
- **Resolution**: 480P, 768P or 1080P

## MiniMax H3 Max editing nodes

| Node | What it does | Starting media |
| :--- | :----------- | :------------- |
| **MiniMax H3 Max Recast** | Replaces the people in a video with people from reference photos, keeping the motion, camera, cuts and sound of the source | 5-30 second video, 1-4 photos |
| **MiniMax H3 Max Extend Video** | Continues a video with new footage described in the prompt | 1.625-60 second video, on H3 Max or H3 Max Turbo |
| **MiniMax H3 Max Reframe Video** | Fits a video into a new aspect ratio and generates the area around it, keeping timing and sound | 0.5-300 second video |
| **MiniMax H3 Max Insert Video** | Replaces the section between two timestamps with a newly generated scene | Video up to 60 seconds, plus a start and resume time |
| **MiniMax H3 Max Lip Sync** | Animates a still image so the mouth follows an audio track | One image, 5-899 seconds of audio |
| **MiniMax H3 Max Camera Control** | Orbits the camera around the subject in a still image | One image, plus an end angle and distance |

In the Recast, Extend Video and Insert Video prompts, refer to the connected references as `@Image1`, `@Audio1` or `@Video1`, numbered in connection order. The Reference to Video node accepts the same shorthand for its own references. A tag that points past the references you connected stops the run with an error.

Rates per node and resolution are in the [Minimax pricing table](/tutorials/partner-nodes/pricing).
