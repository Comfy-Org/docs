Generate sound effects, ambient audio, and foley from text descriptions. Describe the audio you need — from footsteps to thunderstorms — and ElevenLabs produces the matching sound effect.

## Key capabilities

- **Text-prompted sound generation** — Describe the sound, get the audio
- **Ambient environments** — Generate background atmospheres and room tones
- **Foley effects** — Create custom sound effects for video and game production

## Model and settings

The node offers two models, `eleven_sfx_v3` and `eleven_sfx_v2`. The template selects `eleven_sfx_v2`.

- `text`: describe the sound in English, up to 450 characters. Prompts in other languages are often read aloud instead of turned into a sound.
- `duration`: 0.5 to 30 seconds, 5 by default.
- `loop`: generate a seamlessly looping effect.
- `prompt_influence`: 0 to 1, 0.3 by default. Higher values follow the description more closely.
- `output_format`: `mp3_44100_192` or `opus_48000_192`.
