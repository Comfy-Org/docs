Creatify is an AI video ad platform. Three partner nodes bring two of its models into ComfyUI: **Boreal-H3**, an H3 model post-trained for video ads, and **Aurora 2.0**, which turns one photo plus a speech or song clip into a talking or singing video.

All three run on Creatify's servers through the Comfy API. Nothing is downloaded and no local GPU is used; you need a Comfy account with credits. Every render is billed by resolution and length.

<ReqHint/>

<Tip>These nodes need a current ComfyUI build. If they are missing from the node menu, [update ComfyUI](/installation/update_comfyui).</Tip>

## Boreal-H3: Text to Video

Add the **Creatify Boreal-H3 Video** node and describe the ad in the prompt: subject, action, camera, and any spoken lines. Resolution is 768p (default), 1088p, or 2k, and clips run 1 to 15 seconds, or 14 seconds at 2k.

`prompt_enhancement` rewrites your prompt into the form the model works best with. `auto` and `ads` rewrite it as a video ad and can add spoken lines, `general` rewrites it for any other video, and `none` renders it as written.

Connect a first frame to animate it, or a first and last frame to end on a chosen image. A first frame forces `aspect_ratio` to `auto`, because the video follows the image's own ratio.

## Boreal-H3: Reference to Video

Add the **Creatify Boreal-H3 Reference to Video** node to build the scene from reference media. Name each reference in the prompt as `@Image1` to `@Image9`, `@Video1` to `@Video3`, or `@Audio1` to `@Audio3`, in connection order.

- At least one image or video is required. Up to 12 references are accepted in total.
- Reference videos need at least 1 second each and are billed for up to 15 seconds combined. Their sound carries over when the prompt asks to continue it; a new line in the prompt replaces it.
- Reference audio guides the voice instead of dictating the words, and instrumental music can become the soundtrack.
- `aspect_ratio` on `auto` follows the first reference image, or the first reference video when no image is connected.

## Aurora 2.0: Avatar

Add the **Creatify Aurora 2.0 Avatar** node, load one photo and one audio clip, and it returns a talking or singing video as long as the audio, with the audio unchanged.

- The photo needs at least 256 pixels on each side and a ratio between 1:4 and 4:1. The video keeps that ratio.
- Audio runs up to 60 seconds, or 14 seconds at 2k, so use 1080p for longer clips.
- Resolution is 480p, 720p (default), 1080p, or 2k.
- `text_prompt` is optional and can describe the scene, camera, lighting, expression, or motion.

Aurora 2.0 bills per second of audio. Its output is not reproducible: `seed` only decides whether the node re-runs.

## API reference

The nodes mirror the Creatify API endpoints: [Boreal-H3](https://docs.creatify.ai/api-documentation/boreal/model-boreal-h3) and [Aurora 2.0](https://docs.creatify.ai/api-documentation/aurora/model-aurora-2).
