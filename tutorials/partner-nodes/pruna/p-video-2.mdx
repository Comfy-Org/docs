Pruna P-Video-2 is Pruna's quality-focused video generation model, available in ComfyUI through four partner nodes: **Pruna P-Video-2 Text to Video**, **Pruna P-Video-2 Image to Video**, **Pruna P-Video-2 Pro Reference to Video**, and **Pruna P-Video-2 Pro Avatar**. The text and image nodes produce clips from 1 to 20 seconds at 720p or 1080p, at 24 or 48 fps, in seven aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 4:3, 3:4, 3:2, 2:3, 1:1), with native audio: ambience, sound effects, and lip-synced dialogue generated from the prompt.

The image-to-video node animates a first frame with an optional last frame, and both nodes accept an optional audio input that drives the motion and becomes the soundtrack. A draft mode renders faster, less detailed previews billed at 60% of the standard rate, so you can iterate on composition before committing to a full-quality render.

The nodes run entirely on Pruna's servers through the Comfy API. No model downloads or local GPU are required; you need a Comfy API account with credits.

## What P-Video-2 is good at

- **Native audio**: generates a soundtrack with ambience, sound effects, and lip-synced dialogue, or follows a connected audio track
- **Flexible output**: 720p or 1080p at 24 or 48 fps, 1 to 20 seconds (or `auto` duration chosen from the prompt), seven aspect ratios
- **Draft mode**: faster, less detailed previews at 60% of the standard rate
- **First and last frame**: image-to-video animates between two images with closely matched aspect ratios
- **References**: images, videos, and audio drive a Pro clip, addressed as `@Image1`, `@Video1`, and `@Audio1`
- **Talking portraits**: the Avatar node lip-syncs a portrait to a written script or your own recording

See [Pricing](/tutorials/partner-nodes/pricing) for the text to video and image to video rates; the Pro nodes bill per second by resolution and mode, plus the reference media they carry.

### Steps to Run

1. **Enter your prompt**: describe the scene, its motion, and its sound in the **Pruna P-Video-2 Text to Video** node (up to 5000 characters)
2. **Set duration and resolution**: pick 1 to 20 seconds or `auto`, then 720p (about 0.9 megapixels) or 1080p (about 2 megapixels)
3. **Choose the frame rate**: 24 or 48 fps. 48 fps is not available with draft at 1080p
4. **Optional: connect audio**: an audio input drives the motion and sets the video length instead of `duration` (1 to 20 seconds; longer audio is truncated)
5. **Click Queue**: press `Ctrl+Enter` to generate

### Tips

- `enhance_prompt` (on by default) rewrites short prompts with more detail. Turn it off to reproduce a result exactly with the same seed
- `generate_audio` can be turned off for a silent clip

### Steps to Run

1. **Upload a first frame**: connect a **LoadImage** node. The output keeps the aspect ratio of this image
2. **Optional: upload a last frame**: the video ends on this image. Its aspect ratio must be close to the first frame's
3. **Describe the motion and sound**: write how the scene moves and sounds in the prompt (up to 5000 characters)
4. **Set duration, resolution, and frame rate**: same options as text to video
5. **Click Queue**: press `Ctrl+Enter` to generate

## P-Video-2 Pro: Reference to Video

Build a clip from reference material. Add the **Pruna P-Video-2 Pro Reference to Video** node and name each reference in the prompt: `@Image1` to `@Image9`, `@Video1` to `@Video3`, `@Audio1` to `@Audio3`, in connection order.

- Images: up to 9, counting an optional first and last frame. Videos: up to 3, each 2 to 15 seconds at up to 60 fps, downscaled above 1080p. Audio: up to 3 clips. All references together are capped at 12. Videos are capped at 15 seconds in total, and audio at 15 seconds in total.
- `duration` runs from 5 to 14 seconds. `mode` trades price for speed: `speed` renders faster, `cost` is cheaper.
- `aspect_ratio` on `adaptive` follows the ratio most references share. With `enhance_prompt` on, the rewritten prompt picks the ratio instead.
- `keep_audio` uses one connected audio clip as the soundtrack. Audio within a second of `duration` sets the video length, longer audio is cut to `duration`, and shorter audio is followed by generated sound.
- `enhance_prompt` defaults to `turbo`. Keep it on when the prompt refers to references by number, because the rewrite preserves the labels. `off` reproduces a seed exactly, and a first or last frame always uses `turbo`.

No template ships for this node yet. Add it from the node menu and connect your own references.

## P-Video-2 Pro: Avatar

Turn a portrait into a talking video. Add the **Pruna P-Video-2 Pro Avatar** node and choose how the person speaks:

- `script`: the node reads your text with the selected `voice` and `language`. `voice_prompt` sets tone, pacing, and accent, and is not spoken itself.
- `audio`: your own recording is lip-synced. It needs at least 1 second of speech.

The video matches the speech length and is cut at 3 minutes. The portrait keeps its aspect ratio between 1:2 and 4:1; wider or taller images are cropped to 16:9 or 9:16.

`prompt` describes the scene and defaults to `The person is talking.` Resolution is 480p, 768p (default), 1080p, 2k, or 4k, and the three larger sizes upscale the 768p render. `2k` accepts up to 150 seconds of connected audio and `4k` up to 60 seconds.

## Text to video and image to video options

| Option | Values | Default | Notes |
| ------ | ------ | ------- | ----- |
| `duration` | 1 to 20, or auto | 5 | Ignored when audio is connected; the video then follows the audio length |
| `resolution` | 720p / 1080p | 720p | 720p is about 0.9 MP, 1080p about 2 MP at 16:9 |
| `fps` | 24 / 48 | 24 | 48 fps unavailable with draft at 1080p |
| `draft` | on / off | off | Faster, less detailed render at 60% of the standard rate |
| `generate_audio` | on / off | on | Ignored when audio is connected |
| `enhance_prompt` | on / off | on | Advanced. Turn off for exact seed reproducibility |
| `seed` | 0 to 2147483647 | 42 | Exact reproducibility requires enhance_prompt off |
