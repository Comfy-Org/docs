**Idol** is Comfy-Org's fine-tune of TripoSplat. One image of a subject goes in, a 3D Gaussian splat comes out, and stylized characters and props read sharper than they do on the base model.

The workflow runs the same nodes as [TripoSplat](/tutorials/3d/triposplat): only the checkpoints change, so the settings explained there apply here too. It needs ComfyUI 0.23.0 or newer.

## Idol: Image to Gaussian Splat {#3d_idol_image_to_gaussian_splat}

Load one subject on a clean background, then queue. Three branches run at once and write an orbit video, a `.spz` splat and a `.glb` mesh.

## What to change

- **Image to Gaussian Splat** subgraph: `auto_remove_background` runs BiRefNet on the input, so turn it off when the image already has alpha. `num_gaussians` defaults to 262144, which matches the template's point budget; a larger value only oversamples the same surface and costs VRAM and time.
- **seed** is fixed at 46. Change it for variations of the surface detail. The silhouette always comes from the image.
- Model selectors load the Idol checkpoints. Swap in the stock TripoSplat files to compare the two models on the same image.

## Before you run

- One view in, one view out. The front reads well and the sides and back are inferred, so thin parts such as rails, strings or fur strands thin out or pick up colour from the removed backdrop.
- The GLB branch runs SplatToMesh at resolution 384 and returns a multi-million-triangle mesh. It is slow and heavy, so skip it when the splat is the deliverable.
