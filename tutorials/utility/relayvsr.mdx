RelayVSR upscales real footage in one pass. A one-step Wan2.2 5B model writes reference latents every 15th frame, and a small Flash decoder fills the frames between them from those latents and the original low-quality clip. ComfyUI support landed in [ComfyUI #17511](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/pull/17511).

Frame rate and audio pass through untouched, so only the picture is rescaled. The scale factor comes from the checkpoint rather than a widget: a 2x model only works with the 2x VAE, and mixing pairs fails.

The 2x pair was retrained by [LightX2V](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/RelayVSR_X2) for the step after video generation, so a 768 x 1344 clip from MiniMax-H3 comes out at 1536 x 2688. The 4x pair is the original RelayVSR scale.

1. Load your clip in the `Load Video` node. The template trims it to the first 5 seconds with a `Trim Video` node, so raise that duration for longer clips or upscale long footage in passes.
2. Run the workflow. The result is saved by `Save Video` under `ComfyUI/output/video/`.

For 4x, swap the two loader values in the subgraph: `UNETLoader` to `relayvsr_x4_int8_convrot.safetensors` and `VAELoader` to `relayvsr_flash_vae_x4_fp32.safetensors`. Nothing else changes.

If a pass runs out of memory, trim the clip and upscale it in several passes.
