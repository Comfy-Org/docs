## Pricing

Comfy Router charges the model maker's list price, billed from your workspace credits (211 credits = $1 USD). Current per-model prices are on [router.comfy.org](https://router.comfy.org). A workspace with negotiated pricing is quoted the list price and charged its contract rate, and an estimate is not the final charge: what your workspace's usage and billing records settle is what you pay.

- Price a call before you run it: `POST https://api.comfy.org/v2/models/bria/fibo/estimate`.
- Billing facts, such as whether a refused request is charged: the `billing` field from `GET https://api.comfy.org/v2/models/bria/fibo`.
