You can review Comfy Cloud invoices from **Plan & Credits**. **Billing & invoices** opens the Stripe billing portal, where the invoice list and PDF downloads live.

## Accessing Comfy Cloud payment history

1. Visit [cloud.comfy.org](https://cloud.comfy.org/?utm_source=docs) and log in.
2. Open the profile menu and click **Manage plan**.
3. In **Plan & Credits**, click **Billing & invoices**. This opens the Stripe billing portal.
4. Open an invoice in Stripe to view details or download the PDF.

Make sure your billing details are correct before new invoices are generated. Update them from [Edit Comfy Cloud payment information](/support/payment/editing-payment-information).

## When invoices appear

Comfy Cloud issues an invoice each time a payment succeeds, such as a subscription renewal or a credit purchase. Invoices appear in the Stripe billing portal together with your other charges.
