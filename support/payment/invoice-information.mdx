Your invoice is based on the billing details you enter at checkout. Those details appear on later invoices in the Stripe billing portal.

<Card title="Update your payment information" icon="credit-card" href="/support/payment/editing-payment-information">
  Learn how to update your billing details and payment information
</Card>

## See your next invoice

The **Invoices** tab in **Plan & Credits** shows the **Next invoice** amount in USD for your current plan. Open it from the profile menu: **Manage plan** → **Plan & Credits** → **Invoices**.

While the subscription is paused there is no upcoming charge, so the amount is hidden and only the history link remains. If the billing data fails to load, the tab offers a retry.

Invoice history and per-invoice downloads live in the Stripe billing portal. Click **Full invoice history** to open it. Only workspace members who can manage billing see the **Invoices** tab.

## Important notes

- Changes to your payment information only apply to **future invoices**
- We cannot change invoices that have already been issued
- Enter the billing details you want to see on your invoice at checkout
