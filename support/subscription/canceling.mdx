## How to cancel a Comfy Cloud subscription

1. Visit [cloud.comfy.org](https://cloud.comfy.org/?utm_source=docs) and log in.
2. Open the profile menu and click **Manage plan**.
3. In **Plan & Credits**, open the more options menu.
4. Click **Cancel plan**.
5. If the dialog asks why you are cancelling, you can pick a reason or skip it, then click **Continue cancelling**. The reason and the comment are optional. Click **Keep my plan** to stop.
6. In the **Cancel your plan?** dialog, review what you will lose, then click **Cancel my plan** to confirm. Click **Keep my plan** if you change your mind.

Some accounts see a one-time retention offer before the confirmation step. Accepting it keeps your plan at a reduced price for a limited number of payments. Continuing to cancel moves on to the confirmation.

Only workspace members who can manage billing can cancel. After you confirm, you will not be charged again. Features stay active until the end of the current billing period.

You can resume later from **Plan & Credits** with **Resume subscription**. In the subscription pricing table, the same button appears as **Resume** followed by your plan name.

## Important notes

- All Comfy Cloud subscription plans automatically renew unless you set your plan to cancel
- Your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing period
- You can resume your subscription at any time by visiting [cloud.comfy.org](https://cloud.comfy.org/?utm_source=docs)
