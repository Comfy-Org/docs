## How to subscribe

You can browse and use Comfy Cloud normally without a subscription, but you need to subscribe to run workflows.

The **Choose a Plan** dialog has two scopes:

- **For Personal**: individual plans (such as Standard, Creator, and Pro) with a fixed monthly credit amount.
- **For Teams**: a workspace team plan. You set the monthly credit amount with a slider. Larger credit amounts can include a larger discount.

Yearly billing usually costs less per month than monthly.

To subscribe to Comfy Cloud:

1. Visit [cloud.comfy.org](https://cloud.comfy.org/?utm_source=docs) and log in to your account.
2. Click the **Subscribe** button.
3. In the **Choose a Plan** dialog, choose **For Personal** or **For Teams**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="For Personal">
    Personal plans are for individual use. Pick a named plan with a fixed monthly credit amount.

    <img src="/images/support/subscription/pricing-personal.jpg" alt="Choose a Plan dialog with the personal plans" />

    1. Select a billing cycle: **Yearly** or **Monthly**.
    2. Select your desired plan and click **Subscribe**.
    3. Review your order summary, add your payment details, and click **Pay and subscribe** to complete the checkout.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="For Teams">
    Team plans apply to your workspace. You choose how many monthly credits the workspace gets.

    <img src="/images/support/subscription/pricing-team.jpg" alt="Team plan with the credit slider" />

    1. Select a billing cycle: **Yearly** or **Monthly**.
    2. Drag the credit slider to set your monthly credit amount, then click **Subscribe to Team**.
    3. Review your order summary, add your payment details, and click **Pay and subscribe** to complete the checkout.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
Plan prices, credit amounts, and discount rates can change. Use the amounts shown in the **Choose a Plan** dialog or on [comfy.org/cloud/pricing](https://www.comfy.org/cloud/pricing). Screenshots on this page are examples only.
</Note>

Comfy Cloud subscriptions are managed through Stripe, our payment processor.

## Applying a promotion code

If you have a promotion code, you can apply it during checkout to receive a discount on your subscription:

### Step 1: Enter your code

During checkout, type your promotion code into the **Promo code** field and click **Apply**.

<img src="/images/support/subscription/promo-code-1.jpg" alt="Promo code field during checkout" />

### Step 2: Verify the discount

If the code is valid, the discount appears in your order summary and the total updates to reflect it.

<img src="/images/support/subscription/promo-code-3.jpg" alt="Discount applied successfully" />

Complete the payment process to activate your subscription with the applied discount.

<Note>
Promotion codes may have specific terms and conditions, such as expiration dates or usage limits. Make sure to review the details of your promotion code before applying it.
</Note>

## Subscription renewal

Your subscription renews automatically on the same day and time as when you first subscribed. Monthly plans renew each month, and annual plans renew each year.

Annual plans receive their full year of credits up front. All Comfy Cloud subscription plans automatically renew unless you set your plan to cancel.
