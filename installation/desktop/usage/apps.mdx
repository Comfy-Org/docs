An app in Comfy Desktop is a ready-made tool built by Comfy. Each app does one job through a simple interface: you add your image, video, or text and run it, without building a Workflow. Apps open inside Comfy Desktop and run on Comfy Cloud or on your own GPU.

![Apps page in Comfy Desktop with the Search apps box and cards for Remove Background, SDR to HDR, Product Ads, Extend Picture, Character Swap, and Product Swap, followed by the Missing an app? card](/images/desktop/usage/comfy-desktop-apps-page.png)

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Remove Background** | Makes clean cut-outs from any image or video. |
| **SDR to HDR** | Brings standard images into high dynamic range. |
| **Product Ads** | Turns a product shot into ad-ready visuals. |
| **Extend Picture** | Outpaints beyond the original frame. |
| **Character Swap** | Replaces a character in a scene. |
| **Product Swap** | Replaces a product in a scene. |

<Note>
  Desktop Apps aren't the **Apps** tab inside ComfyUI, which lists your own saved app Workflows, or [App Mode](/interface/app-mode). See [the ComfyUI Apps tab](/interface/apps).
</Note>

## Find an app

Open the **Apps** page in one of these ways:

- In the **sidebar**, click **Apps**.
- On **Home**, click **See all apps** below the row of apps.

![Apps page: the sidebar shows Home, Apps selected, Assets, Builds and Comfy Cloud, and the gallery lists Remove Background, SDR to HDR, Product Ads, Extend Picture, Character Swap and Product Swap](/images/desktop/usage/comfy-desktop-apps-sidebar.png)

Each app is a card with its name. Hold the pointer over a card to read what the app does. To find an app, type in **Search apps** at the top right. Search matches an app's name and description. The page has no categories or filters.

On **Home**, the row of apps turns when you click a card on either side. Click the card in the middle to open that app.

## Run an app

1. Click an app's card. The app opens in the Comfy Desktop window, next to the **sidebar**.
2. In the panel on the left, add your input. Click **Examples** to start from a sample, or import your own image or video. Some apps also ask for a prompt or a model.
3. Click **Choose where to run**, and then select **Comfy Cloud** or **This computer**. See [Where apps run](#where-apps-run).
4. Click the button at the bottom of the panel. Its name follows the app, such as **Generate** or **Remove background**.

The **Examples** tab on the right shows sample results for the app. Click **Create** to see your own results.

![Remove Background app in Comfy Desktop: All apps and the app name at the top left, the Your picture or video panel with Examples and Import image or video, Choose where to run and the Remove background button at the bottom, and the Examples tab on the right with cut-out results](/images/desktop/usage/comfy-desktop-remove-background-app.png)

An app with more inputs, such as **Character Swap**, takes a character image, a video, a prompt, a model, and a quality setting:

![Character Swap app in Comfy Desktop with Your character, Your video, Prompt, Model, and Quality in the left panel, Choose where to run and Generate at the bottom, and before and after examples on the right](/images/desktop/usage/comfy-desktop-character-swap.png)

## Switch or leave an app

- Click **All apps** at the top left of the app, or **Apps** in the **sidebar**, to go back to the list.
- While an app is open, its name appears under **Apps** in the **sidebar**. Click the name to return to the app where you left it.

A window holds one app at a time. Opening another app replaces the one that's open. Links that lead outside the app open in your browser.

## Where apps run

**Choose where to run** offers two places:

![Choose where to run menu open above the Generate button, with Comfy Cloud, Runs on Comfy's cloud GPUs, and This computer, Runs on your own GPU](/images/desktop/usage/comfy-desktop-apps-where-to-run.png)

- **Comfy Cloud**: the app runs on Comfy's cloud GPUs. It needs your Comfy account, and the app asks you to log in if you haven't. See [Log in and open Comfy Cloud](/installation/desktop/overview#log-in-and-open-comfy-cloud).
- **This computer**: the app runs on your own GPU, using one of your local builds. Comfy Desktop picks the build the app asks for, else a running build, else the build you used last, and it skips builds older than the ComfyUI version the app needs. It starts that build in the background without a ComfyUI window. A build that started only for an app stops after 10 minutes with nothing to do.

Apps don't run on remote builds or Legacy Desktop builds. To add a local build, see [Builds](/installation/desktop/usage/instance-management#install-a-new-build).

## Credits

Browsing and opening apps is free. What a run costs depends on the models the app uses and where it runs:

- An app built on open models is free to run on your own GPU.
- An app that uses partner models spends Comfy Credits, wherever it runs.
- A run on Comfy Cloud spends Comfy Credits, the same as any Comfy Cloud run.

Your **Credits** balance at the bottom of the **sidebar** refreshes when you close an app. For plans and pricing, see [Comfy Cloud](/get_started/cloud).

## Open an app's Workflow in ComfyUI

An app can open the Workflow behind it in ComfyUI, so you can change it yourself. Use the app's own control for this. Comfy Desktop starts the build if it isn't running, opens its ComfyUI window, and loads the Workflow there. The Workflow opens on Comfy Cloud or on a local build, depending on where the app ran.

## Save and view results

When an app shows a result, Comfy Desktop opens it in a viewer:

- **Download** saves a copy when the file isn't on this computer yet.
- **Show in Finder**, **Show in Explorer**, or **Show in folder** opens the file's folder when it's on this computer.
- **Open workflow** opens the Workflow that made the file.
- **View in Assets** opens a Comfy Cloud result on the [Assets](/installation/desktop/usage/assets) page.

When an app running on this computer needs a model, the download appears in the [Downloads](/installation/desktop/usage/downloads) tray. Comfy Desktop only downloads `.safetensors` models from Hugging Face for apps.

## Ask for an app

The last card on the **Apps** page reads **Missing an app?**

1. Click **Ask for an app**.
2. In **What app do you want?**, describe the app in up to 500 characters.
3. Click **Send**.

The dialog reads **Thanks. We got your request.** Your request helps decide which apps Comfy builds next.

## Troubleshoot

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Apps don't appear in the sidebar">
    Apps aren't available for your Comfy Desktop yet. Comfy turns them on, and there's no setting for them. **Home** shows example Workflows instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Apps didn't load">
    The **Apps** page reads **Apps didn't load**, or **Home** reads **Apps couldn't be loaded. Check your connection and try again.** Check your internet connection, and then click **Try again**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An app didn't load">
    The app reads **{app} didn't load** and **Check your internet connection, then try again.** Click **Try again**, or click **All apps** to go back to the list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An app can't run on this computer">
    The app shows one of these messages:

    - **Add a local build in Comfy Desktop to run apps on this computer.** [Install a build](/installation/desktop/usage/instance-management#install-a-new-build), or run the app on Comfy Cloud.
    - **This app needs ComfyUI {version} or newer. Update a local build in Comfy Desktop.** [Update ComfyUI](/installation/desktop/usage/build-management#update-comfyui) in a build.
    - **{name} is still installing. Try again once it finishes.** Wait for the install on **Home**, and then run the app again.
    - **Comfy Desktop could not start {name}.** See [Fix a build that fails](/installation/desktop/usage/build-troubleshooting#fix-a-build-that-fails).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Search finds nothing">
    The page reads **Nothing matches**. Try another word, or click **Clear search**. To ask for an app that doesn't exist yet, click **Ask for an app**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
