Comfy Desktop can install a ComfyUI setup that your team shares on the Comfy Developer Platform, such as one approved build with chosen models and Custom Nodes. On the Developer Platform, this setup is a Developer Platform Build that your workspace publishes in numbered versions, and Comfy Desktop installs one version on your computer as a team build. You choose the workspace, install its build, update it when your team publishes a newer version, and upload your own builds to the Developer Platform.

To create a Build on the Developer Platform and publish its versions, see [Adding Custom Builds to Comfy Cloud](/development/deploy/cloud-custom-builds).

## Choose the workspace whose builds you see

Each workspace has its own Developer Platform Builds, and Comfy Desktop shows one workspace at a time. To see your team's builds, log in first. Follow [Log in and open Comfy Cloud](/installation/desktop/overview#log-in-and-open-comfy-cloud).

The workspace selected at the bottom of the **sidebar** decides what you see:

- **Home**, the **sidebar**, the **Builds** page, and build pages list only the builds in that workspace.
- **New build** offers that workspace's Developer Platform Builds under **Your team's builds**. Your **Personal** workspace has its own.

To switch workspaces, see [Use your workspace menu](/installation/desktop/usage/overview#use-your-workspace-menu). Comfy Desktop remembers the workspace you select and opens on it next time.

<Tip>
  When your team publishes a build while Comfy Desktop is open, click **Refresh workspaces** on the **Builds** page. It reloads your workspaces and their Developer Platform Builds.
</Tip>

## Install a build from your team

A Developer Platform Build appears in Comfy Desktop once it has a published version with a build for your computer's operating system and GPU. Installing it adds a team build on this computer. For the parts of the **New build** dialog that every build shares, such as **Name** and **Location**, see [Install a new build](/installation/desktop/usage/instance-management#install-a-new-build).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New build dialog">
    On the **Builds** page, click **New build**.

    When you're logged in, the dialog opens on your workspace's builds, with the first build selected. If the workspace has no build to install, it opens on **Official release** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the build and hardware">
    The summary shows what installs:

    - **Release**: the build's name, with its description, **Release v{version}**, and **By {creator}**.
    - **Hardware**: the build target for this computer's operating system and GPU, such as NVIDIA with CUDA 12.8, with its release version.

    The **Name** box suggests the build's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick another build (optional)">
    Click **Change** to open **Change setup**.

    - **Source**: select **Your team's builds**. If you aren't logged in, your browser opens so you can log in, and the dialog shows **Continue in your browser**.
    - **Release**: select a build. The list shows only builds that can install on this computer. Click **View builds online** to open your workspace's builds on the Developer Platform in your browser, or click **Refresh builds** to reload the list.
    - **Hardware**: select a build target. The recommended target for this computer is selected for you.

    While Comfy Desktop switches to the workspace, the dialog reads **Waiting for workspace authorization… Complete authorization in your browser if prompted.**

    Click **Save changes** to go back to the summary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the build">
    Click **Install**. To follow the install, see [Install a new build](/installation/desktop/usage/instance-management#install-a-new-build).
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the install finishes, the build's tile on the **Builds** page shows **Build v{version}**, the version you installed. If the build isn't in the list, see [Troubleshoot](#troubleshoot).

## Update a team build

A team build moves only between versions of its Developer Platform Build. When your team publishes a newer version, update the build to get it. Its build page shows a **Build version** section on the **Overview** tab.

1. In the **sidebar**, click the team build to open its build page. When an update is available, the **Overview** tab shows a dot, and the build's tile shows **Update available**.
2. On **Overview**, check the **Build version** section: **Installed**, **Latest published**, and **Last checked**, with **Update available** or **Up to date**. To check again, click **Check for updates**.
3. Click **Update to v{version}**. You can also select **Update** in the [quick actions menu](/installation/desktop/usage/instance-management#quick-actions-from-the-build-list).
4. In the **Update build** dialog, check the versions it moves between, and then click **Update now**.

The update replaces the build's environment with the new version and keeps your Workflows and the models already staged. While it runs, its progress reads **Updating to v{version}**. When it finishes, **Installed** shows the new version.

## Upload a build to the Developer Platform

**Upload build** starts a Developer Platform Build from a build on this computer, so your workspace can publish it and your team can install it. You need to be logged in, and the build must be installed on this computer. Comfy Cloud and remote builds can't be uploaded.

1. Right-click the build in the **sidebar**, or right-click its tile on the **Builds** page or its card on **Home**.
2. Select **Upload build**. Its hint reads **Upload this build to Comfy Developer Platform.**

While it runs, the item reads **Creating…**. Comfy Desktop then:

1. Saves a manual snapshot of the build. It appears on the build's **Snapshots** tab. See [Save and restore snapshots](/installation/desktop/usage/build-snapshots#save-and-restore-snapshots).
2. Sends the snapshot to the Developer Platform as a draft, in the workspace the build belongs to. A build in your **Personal** workspace goes to your **Personal** workspace.
3. Opens the draft in your browser, where the build page shows **Draft** and **Desktop snapshot received**.

The draft doesn't appear under **Your team's builds** until a version of it is published and finishes building on the Developer Platform. To publish it, see [Publish the Build](/development/deploy/cloud-custom-builds#publish-the-build).

If another task runs on the build, **Upload build** is grayed out. Hold the pointer over it to read **Wait for the current task on this build to finish.**

## Troubleshoot

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Release reads No compatible Builds are available to install.">
    Comfy Desktop lists a Developer Platform Build only when its latest published version finished building and has a build for this computer's operating system and GPU. Check that the workspace selected in the **sidebar** is the one your team publishes to. Click **View builds online** to check the build on the Developer Platform, and then click **Refresh builds**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The dialog shows Couldn't load builds. Retry">
    Comfy Desktop couldn't load your workspace's builds. Click **Couldn't load builds. Retry** to load them again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Could not upload build">
    The **Could not upload build** alert gives the reason, such as **Could not create a draft in Comfy Developer Platform.** Select **Upload build** again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
